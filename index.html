The Journey

In 2015, the love for skating caught on fast. Madrollers started with a handful of members in Dubai, then Abu Dhabi — and has grown into one of the region's largest skating communities. Over 1600 skate sessions delivered, 2500+ members from 40+ nationalities, and a network stretching across 15 countries.

2015

Founded in Dubai

Music × Art × Sport

Funkyy Rollers

Launched in Dubai in 2024 as a new roller sports concept, Funkyy Rollers is Madrollers' subsidiary club — built at the intersection of music, art, sport and community. Designed to introduce skating to a broader audience through a lifestyle-oriented experience, complementing Madrollers' grassroots and competitive focus.
